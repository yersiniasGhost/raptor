Scenario A: Battery Priority

✓
Batteries Charged - Handle Load from Battery
This mode prioritizes battery power to handle the electrical load. AC input is disabled to force battery operation. The system monitors battery voltage and current while providing low voltage protection. Ideal when you want to preserve grid independence or during peak rate periods.

Scenario B: Split Load

✓
Low Batteries - Split Load (50% Battery + 50% AC)
This balanced mode splits the electrical load between battery power and AC input. AC consumption is limited to 50% of the total load, forcing the battery to provide the remainder. Perfect for extending battery life during moderate discharge periods while maintaining some grid independence.

Scenario C: AC Only

✓
AC Only - Batteries Disconnected
Full AC power mode where the grid handles the entire electrical load. Battery charging is disabled and battery interaction is minimized. The system operates essentially as a pass-through with batteries in standby. Use during battery maintenance or when maximum grid utilization is desired.

Scenario D: AC + Charging

✓
AC for Load + Battery Charging
Comprehensive AC mode where grid power handles the load while simultaneously charging the batteries. Charging parameters are optimized based on battery voltage with automatic boost or float mode selection. Ideal for battery recovery periods or when excess grid capacity is available.
